# WereCode

WereCode is the product context for Octave, a guitar-learning system that helps a learner turn a song they know by ear into something they can understand, practice, and play.

## Language

**Octave**:
The system around the learner's song: it gives the experience its musical evidence, playback surfaces, song artifacts, and product workflow.
_Avoid_: App, workstation, bench

**Maestro**:
The AI music coach and narrative protagonist of Octave. Maestro understands a song as music and guides the learner through playing, explaining, practicing, and arranging it for guitar.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, assistant, helper

**Player Past the Basics**:
A guitar learner who can already play some chords and has enough musical context to recognize notes, chords, tabs or sheet music, and multi-instrument arrangements. This is a target learner profile, not a strict capability cutoff.
_Avoid_: Absolute beginner, day-one learner, pro musician

**Guitar-First**:
Octave understands the whole recording so Maestro can guide the guitarist. Other instruments matter as musical context, but the primary product promise is about getting the song onto guitar.
_Avoid_: All-instrument learning app, generic music AI

**Learner Context**:
What Maestro knows about the learner from their questions, doubts, and progress around a song, with broader history across songs becoming more important over time.
_Avoid_: Profile settings, preference form
